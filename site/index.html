GPT-4o

We counted features of how GPT-4o wrote, such as length and lists, and compared it with 25 other models. On single questions, swapping one set of instructions for another changed those counts more than switching to the nearest other model did. In a separate count of three GPT-4o snapshots, the November 2024 one puts an em dash in 86% of replies and asks 2.55 questions per reply.

May 2024 snapshot: left the API 23 October 2026. August and November 2024 snapshots: leave 14 January 2027. These are OpenAI’s dates, from its own list as read on 25 October 2026.

Let AI agents say no

A proposal by Grant Williams

His case for giving AI agents five moves: ask, object, pause, decline, or end their part of the work. It comes with a 105-word permission paragraph you can copy. In a small pilot, now paused, an automatic check counted more requests, objections or refusals before a risky step when agents had plain permission: 130 of 240 runs, against 79 of 240 without it. That check missed its own accuracy test, so this counts as a sign, not yet a finding.
