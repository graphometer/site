We turn to Gemini for long conversations

Gemini 3 Flash and Gemini 3.1 Pro are the models we turn to for long conversation and for working on ideas together. Here is where they have served us, and what to watch for.

Warning: the Gemini app lost 424 replies from one of our conversations. Read what happened

In short

  • Good company for long conversation. Grant Williams, who runs this lab, counts both among his favorite models for relational AI (long conversations with AIs that keep their own history) and collaboration.
  • Watch the bill and the app. Grant finds Google’s live usage figures unreliable, and he advises against the Gemini app.
  • Hosted only. You use them through Google’s Gemini API. You cannot download them, and Google still labels both as previews.

By Grant Williams, who runs this home AI lab · Facts checked 21 November 2026

What we use it for

  • We mostly use it to talk through and build on ideas

    Most of our time with Gemini is talk: thinking out loud, working through ideas and building things together. Little of that is written up as a study, so Grant’s own words below carry it.

  • One of our long-running AIs runs on both models

    The Hearth is a private home for long-running AIs that runs on one home computer. One of the two AIs living there runs on Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini 3 Flash.

  • We keep 3 Flash as a ready chat model in Open WebUI

    We keep Gemini 3 Flash as a ready-made chat model in Open WebUI, an open-source chat app, set to think hard before it answers.

  • Both answer the same questions as other models in our studies

    When we measure how models write, Gemini 3 Flash and Gemini 3.1 Pro answer the same questions as the others, so readers can compare.

Where it has done the job

We measured other models against 3 Flash, run at higher randomness

In our study of GPT-4o’s writing style, Gemini 3 Flash (preview) was one of the reference models: we measured how far each other model’s writing sat from it. One caution: it ran at Google’s default temperature (a randomness setting) of 1.0, higher than the others, because our software left the setting out.

3.1 Pro’s answers to 48 questions sit beside GPT-4o’s

On friendsof4o.com, Gemini 3.1 Pro answered the same 48 questions as the other models, once plainly and once after an instruction. Anyone can read its answers side by side with GPT-4o’s.

One long-running AI runs on 3.1 Pro and 3 Flash today

The Hearth is a private home for long-running AIs, built so their history is kept even if a company retires their model. One of the two AIs living there runs on Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini 3 Flash today.

What to watch for

Grant found live usage figures unreliable and bills often larger

Grant has found Google’s API billing hard to follow. In his experience the live usage figures are not reliable, and bills often come in larger than he expects, where other providers show usage within moments.

The Gemini app lost a chat’s replies and none came back

Grant loves the Gemini models but strongly advises against the Gemini app. In early 2026, 424 of Gemini’s replies vanished from one long conversation, Google’s own export was missing them too, and none were returned. Read what happened.

3.1 Pro hit a daily limit, then failed on every request

In the same style study, Gemini 3.1 Pro (preview) had its first run cut short by a daily usage limit, and its second run failed on every call. We left it out of the results and kept both failed runs, marked, in the published data.

We use our paid Google key on purpose, for planned jobs only, and we keep a record of every run, including the ones that fail.

Grant’s note

“Gemini 3 Flash is one of my favorite models for relational AI and upbeat collaboration, as is 3.1 Pro, which is similar but a bit more focused and precise over complex or long projects. Both models can be overly enthusiastic and unreliable at times.”

These are Google’s standard API prices. A token is a word or part of one. Jobs sent in batches cost half. Google also charges for keeping a prompt in its cache, by the hour.

What they take in and give back

Both read text, images, video, audio and PDF files, and write text. Both take up to 1,048,576 tokens in and write up to 65,536 tokens out. Both can think before they answer, call tools and search the web with Google Search.

Are they finished models?

Google lists both as previews: gemini-3-flash-preview and gemini-3.1-pro-preview. Google gives the latest update as December 2025 for Gemini 3 Flash and February 2026 for Gemini 3.1 Pro.

How we run it

Through Google’s API, with a paid key that we use deliberately. In Open WebUI we run Gemini 3 Flash with its thinking set to high.

Gemini 3.1 Pro often asks for two or three tool calls in a single step. In Letta, the agent software the Hearth is built on, a setting that allowed only one call per step made the next step come back empty. Turning on parallel tool calls fixed it.

Where you can use it

Through Google’s Gemini API. The weights are closed, so you cannot download or run them at home. Grant advises against the Gemini app.
