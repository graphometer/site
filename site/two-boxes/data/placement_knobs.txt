Placement knobs and the three named profiles, quoted from the installed configuration's own
comment block. Knob names carry a service prefix in the original; it is replaced here, and the
knob that was named after this machine is written <KNOB_RAM_LAYERS>. Three clauses were removed:
one naming other software that runs beside the model on the card, one naming other tenants of the
laptop, and one naming a network product in the bind policy. Nothing with a number was altered.

PLACEMENT KNOBS (layers 0..42): <KNOB_GPU_FULL> layers live whole on the card; layers up to
<KNOB_RAM_LAYERS>-1 keep attention on the card with experts in RAM; the rest go whole to the
<LAPTOP>. Card about 2.2 GB per whole layer + about 6 GB fixed; <LAPTOP> about 2.25 GB per layer
(108 GiB ceiling); RAM about 2.1 GB per expert layer. The three named profiles:
  gated default   <KNOB_RAM_LAYERS>=18 <KNOB_GPU_FULL>=8  -> card 22 GB | RAM 25 GB | <LAPTOP> 56 GB   (16-17 t/s, 185 prefill)
  card-light      <KNOB_RAM_LAYERS>=18 <KNOB_GPU_FULL>=4  -> card 13 GB | RAM 33 GB | <LAPTOP> 56 GB
  <LAPTOP>-heavy  <KNOB_RAM_LAYERS>=13 <KNOB_GPU_FULL>=8  -> card 22 GB | RAM 12 GB | <LAPTOP> 67 GB   (same decode, prefill 160)

ALLOWED VALUES (the script validates <KNOB_CTX>; anything else refuses): 32768 | 65536 | 131072

The same file records the model it serves as "284B MoE, 256 experts, 6 active + 1 shared, arch
deepseek4", and the 3-bit file as 96.9 GiB, which is that file's own round of the 97.05 GiB the
shard byte counts add up to.

The sweep runs' draft length is not in this file. It is in the headers of the two draft runs
(logs/deepseek-draft-runs-and-maverick.log, "+ DSpark draft (n-max 8)"), while the installed
service reports the draft model's own n_max=6 (installed-4bit-preset-run.txt).

Profile names are the configuration's own. "gated default" there means the profile that passed
the tool-and-recall check, which is how the page words it.
